DS-08: Staff App Wireframes

Approved, 1 Nov 2026. WinForms client of the Staff API (PB-46, PB-47). Traces to FR04, FR07, FR08; UC.04, UC.07, UC.08. Sample data is illustrative.

Common rules

  • One window with three tabs. The title bar shows the store and staff name from the pre-issued token.
  • All calls are asynchronous; the status bar shows loading, errors and the last refresh time.
  • 401 shows "Token invalid or expired"; 403 shows "This role cannot use this function"; connection errors keep the screen and offer Retry.
